## Transfer to ticket interaction

Use the source as a visual/physical hanging reference: restrained gray line, one silver center clip per long ticket, small settling rotation and calm spring arrival, viewport-top hide/reveal. Retain actual tearing as a separate discrete paper-perforation mechanic, since this video has no tear.

For a physically coherent ticket variant the clip should remain fixed to rope throughout drag; ticket pivots below that anchor. The line can deform a few pixels while dragging, then settle quickly. Avoid scale deformation of paper and large rubber-band sag. On detachment, the main ticket should recoil by only a few degrees and the stub should carry the velocity at the final perforation break.

This is visual-level analysis, not evidence of pixel-level replication. Source capture includes editorial camera moves, so matching their absolute pixel motion as interaction physics would be misleading.
